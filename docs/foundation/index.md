# Community Support for the Gonka Foundation

12 November 2026. Text proposal, no on-chain messages.

## Summary

The Gonka Foundation (Gonka Stiftung) is a Swiss foundation based in Zug (CHE-232.447.079), established to support the long-term development, neutrality and resilience of Gonka.

The Foundation asks the community to endorse it as the foundation supporting Gonka. This text proposal gives the community a voice on the Foundation’s role; the Foundation’s statutes and the Board’s duties under Swiss law remain unchanged.

Funding of the Foundation will be submitted as a separate proposal.

## Background

Compute, and above all inference, is becoming one of the world’s most important resources, yet access to it is concentrated in a handful of providers. Gonka is a decentralized network where GPU owners supply AI compute, giving developers, companies and governments access to AI inference (and, soon, training) that no single provider controls. The network currently processes approximately 120 billion AI tokens per day (source: [gonka.gg explorer, Inference Timeline](https://gonka.gg/network/inference?tab=inference)), among the highest volumes measured across decentralized inference networks.

As the network has grown, so has the need for a legal entity able to contract in support of it. Exchanges, hardware vendors, enterprise customers and public institutions require a contractual counterparty. Agreements with contractors funded by the community also need a party able to enter into and enforce them in its own name. At present, the network has no such entity.

## Mission and scope

The Foundation’s mission is to help make AI compute open. The Foundation will act, in its own name, as a legal counterparty in support of the Gonka ecosystem, appoint an Executive Director and a team, and:

- steward the Gonka brand, trademarks and open-source commitments for the benefit of the community, to the extent such rights are held by or licensed to the Foundation;
- support open protocol research, development and grants;
- bring together hosts, developers, researchers and public institutions;
- publish governance records, financial reports and network data in plain, checkable form;
- engage with policymakers, standards bodies and the open-source community.

Protocol parameters and upgrades remain subject to on-chain governance. The Foundation does not operate nodes and does not vote with network weight.

## Legal status

The Foundation was established by Bitfury and entered in the [Commercial Register of the Canton of Zug](https://zg.chregister.ch/cr-portal/auszug/auszug.xhtml?uid=CHE-232.447.079) on 13 October 2026. Its launch was announced on 30 October 2026. The Foundation is governed by Swiss law, is audited by Wadsack Zug AG and is subject to the Swiss foundation supervisory authority. Under its statutes, the Foundation supports the Gonka Protocol “without seeking to exercise exclusive control” over it.

The Foundation’s bank account is in the process of being opened. The Foundation currently holds no funds and has no staff. If the proposal does not pass, the Board will review the Foundation’s role with the community and report back.

The Foundation’s official website is currently <https://gonka.org>. The full website will be launched shortly.

Official records: [Commercial Register of the Canton of Zug](https://zg.chregister.ch/cr-portal/auszug/auszug.xhtml?uid=CHE-232.447.079); [Zefix, the federal company index](https://www.zefix.admin.ch/en/search/entity/list/firm/1772357), including the publication in the Swiss Official Gazette of Commerce.

## Board of the Foundation

The Foundation’s Board was appointed by its founder and currently consists of Dr. Michael Martin, Brian Behlendorf and Benedikt Kukačka, as registered in the Swiss Commercial Register.

Board fees will be disclosed in aggregate in each quarterly report. The Foundation intends to expand the Board to five members. Candidates for new Board seats will be presented to the community for consultation, which may include a non-binding on-chain vote, before the Board makes its appointment.

## Commitments to the community

The Foundation commits to the following:

1. New Board members will be announced before they take office. New Board members are appointed in line with the statutes. Before any appointment, the Board will ask the community for suggestions and feedback and give that input due weight. As a matter of policy, the Board intends to keep to five members and will seek the community’s input before any further expansion.
2. The Foundation will publish a quarterly report on expenditure, staffing, contracts and results, together with its audited annual accounts, on the Foundation’s official website.
3. The Foundation will hold its funds in fiat currency, stablecoins and GNK with regulated custodians, in accordance with a treasury policy approved by the Board.
4. The Foundation is committed to practicing competent treasury management, which includes carefully considering the impact upon the market of converting its finite GNK reserves (granted from the Community Pool) into cash to fund operations.
5. The Foundation commits to engagement and transparency in its spending plans, so as to ensure a lean operation and minimize the drawdown of reserves.
