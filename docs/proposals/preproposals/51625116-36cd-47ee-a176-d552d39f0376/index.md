## Problem

Gonka has funded three offline appearances so far. Every one of them was a business conference.

- **#42** – a scientific panel at the Global Compute Sovereignty Summit in Tashkent. 10,000 USDT. Passed 68.6% with zero NO and zero VETO.
- **#51** – Platinum presence at WebX Asia in Tokyo. 75,000 USDT. Passed 62.8%.
- Token2049 Singapore – sponsor of a partner side-summit.

Ministers, funds, chipmakers, integrators. All valuable. All B2B.

In none of them did the network do the one thing a decentralized network is supposed to do: **put its own community in the same room.** Not a booth for strangers – a room for hosts, miners, pool participants, developers and holders who have been running this network together for a year and have never once shaken hands.

And there is a second gap. By public governance records and public announcements, Gonka has never held a funded event anywhere in Russia – the region where the largest and most active share of the community actually lives. The Russian-speaking segment maintains its own chat, its own knowledge bases, its own mining pools, and shows up in almost every community initiative on this platform. It has never been visited.

Critics of Gonka have used the CIS concentration as an argument *against* the project. This proposal turns it into an asset instead.

## Proposal

Allocate **20,000 USDT** from the Community Pool so Gonka becomes the AI Partner of **Omniverse Web3 Camp** – a two-day digital-culture festival on August 25–26, 2026 at the Brusnitsyn creative cluster in St. Petersburg: 3,000 m² of festival area, 1,000 m² EXPO, a 200 m² terrace lounge on the Gulf of Finland.

This is not a logo on a banner. It is a fully built, branded Gonka space plus stage time plus the festival's marketing surface.

## Why this matters to the network, not just the brand

**GPU capacity.** Russia and the CIS have cheap power and a mature mining and datacenter industry that is actively looking for what comes after Bitcoin ASICs. These are exactly the people Gonka needs: operators who already own racks, already understand uptime, and can bring real H100-class capacity on-chain. A terrace lounge is where that conversation happens over two days, repeatedly, with the same people coming back – not in a 90-second booth pitch.

**Developers.** Two stage slots and a booth in front of an audience that builds. Track 2 of the approved Network Development Roadmap is developer and AI-agent access; this is direct distribution for it.

**Demand activation (Track 11).** The site banner can point at whatever the community decides matters most – a hackathon, a grant call, an onboarding funnel – and it is measurable.

**Community coordination (Track 12).** Governance participation on this network is thin. Proposal #85 died on a 7.6% turnout. Communities that have met in person vote. That is not a slogan, it is the cheapest governance fix available.

## People, not handles

The part that does not fit in a KPI table.

Everyone here has been a wallet address to everyone else for a year. Miners who debug each other's nodes at 3 a.m. have never met. The team is a set of GitHub avatars and a voice on a podcast.

The terrace lounge is deliberately informal – a bar, seating, the Gulf of Finland, DJ sets, no badge hierarchy. Two days where hosts, developers, pool operators and the team talk as people. You are part of this community; come be in it.

That is a strange thing to put a number on. It is also the thing every durable network eventually does, and Gonka has not done it yet.

## Commitments

We know the pattern this community is tired of: money out the door, no way to check what came back. Proposal #74 said it plainly – 300k+ USDT of marketing spend with no unified way to verify results. So:

1. **The build happens in public.** From the moment this is approved until the doors open, production of the Gonka zone is documented in the festival channel – [t.me/omniversefest](https://t.me/omniversefest). Renders, construction, branding, stage lineup. You do not wait until September to find out whether the money turned into anything; you watch it turn into something.
2. **Public post-event report** in GitHub Discussions within 14 days: expense breakdown, attendance, contacts collected, media pickup, photo and video. Same reporting commitment 6Block made in #51.
3. **On-chain and traceable.** Standard `withdraw_ibc` from the community-sale contract, same execution path as #42 and #51. One address, publicly visible.
4. **No MLM. No pool-selling. No referral schemes** anywhere in the Gonka zone or on the Gonka stage. This community has been dealing with pyramid schemes attaching themselves to Gonka's name for months. The Gonka space at this festival will carry official information and official onboarding only.
5. **Tickets go to the community**, not to the organizer's guest list.
6. **This is not our first one.** The same team ran 0xFest last year – the video recap and photos are on the final page of the deck. The venue, the media pipeline and the influencer network for this festival are already contracted.
