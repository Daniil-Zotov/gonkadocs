## Full Proposal

What does your team plan to build or deliver for Gonka over the next three months?

Over the next three months, Team EntroPi plans to continue developing a **decentralized cognitive orchestration layer**.

The system is designed to decompose complex goals into executable tasks, route them across different types of AI infrastructure, and improve execution over time through reinforcement learning.

The infrastructure layer is intended to work across:

* Local AI infrastructure.
* Cloud AI infrastructure.
* Sovereign AI infrastructure.
* Decentralized AI infrastructure.

The goal is to create an orchestration layer that can coordinate AI task execution across multiple environments and make decentralized AI workflows more efficient and adaptive.
