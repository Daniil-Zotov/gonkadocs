Approve DevShard v5 and contributor bounty payouts

This proposal adds DevShard v5 to the approved list of devshard versions and distributes 91,300 USDT from the community-sale contract to contributors.

Release was tested with core testteam and external testlab.

DevShard v5 is the next protocol version after the v4 line. Its main protocol addition is height-sync, which gives DevShard sessions a signed, replayable view of mainnet height and establishes protocol-level logical time for future cPoC, timeout, dispute mechanisms and protocol-level QoS.

The release also includes high-availability improvements, warm cutover and session recovery, Gateway updates, validation and settlement fixes, security hardening, observability improvements and expanded end-to-end testing.

The bounty recipients are Gonka core-contributors who have been actively involved across multiple development cycles and releases:

@akup / @a-kuprin — contributing since v0.2.6, and extensively across DevShard v3, v4 and v5.
@qdanik — active since v0.2.11, including v0.2.14/v0.2.15 and DevShard/Gateway work.
@Ryanchen911 — active since the v0.2.12 cycle, with contributions across chain, security and DevShard-related releases.
@aikuznetsov — active through the DevShard v4 / v0.2.15 cycle, primarily on testing and protocol reliability.
@snevolin — active since the DevShard v4 / v0.2.15 cycle, focused on high availability, routing and safe runtime upgrades.
@shd — contributed to DevShard v4/v5 protocol review and formalization, including the height-sync design.
@redstartechno — active since the v0.2.15 development cycle, contributing reliability and DevShard fixes.

The detailed technical scope, individual contributions, PR references and full bounty breakdown are available here: <https://github.com/gonka-ai/gonka/pull/1584>
