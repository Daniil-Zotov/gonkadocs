The Gonka Marketing Committee requests 6,800 USDT + 50,000 GNK from the Community Pool to support ongoing Committee work and a separate two-month Reddit Contributor Pilot.

The Committee acts as a community-side contributor team supporting active marketing projects. Its work includes joining contractor calls, reviewing deliverables, tracking progress, and consolidating feedback from hosts and other contributors. The team has already been involved with TheSoul and Input, including reviews of Gonka positioning, the Brandbook, website wireframes, and other ongoing materials.

The proposal also includes a two-month Reddit pilot to test a more systematic community presence on the platform. This includes developing r/GonkaHub, bringing in contributors, identifying relevant communities, and supporting active participation.
