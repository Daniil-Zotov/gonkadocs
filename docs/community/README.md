# Gonka Community

Welcome to the Gonka community hub. This section brings together governance committees, discussions, roadmap, and everything you need to participate in the network's development.

---

## Governance Committees

Gonka governance is supported by specialized committees that help evaluate proposals, coordinate execution, and ensure transparency.

### [Gonka Product Committee](./gonka%20product%20committee/README.md)

Evaluates proposals for product value, risks, and roadmap alignment. Publishes scored assessments using the **Proposal Scoring Framework v1**.

- **Focus:** Product & technical proposals, roadmap alignment, risk detection
- **Lead:** [@paranko](https://telegram.me/paranko)
- **Format:** Recorded sessions, AMAs, GIP meetings

---

### [Governance Support Committee](./governance%20support%20committee/README.md)

A service committee that makes the governance process clear, transparent, and convenient for proposal authors, hosts, and the community. Does not evaluate proposals — supports the process.

- **Tasks:** On-chain voting support, proposal discussion initiation, AMA organization, status tracking, deadline monitoring
- **Composition:** 9 members including hosts and contributors

---

### [Go-to-Market Committee](./go-to-market%20committee/README.md)

Develops go-to-market direction: from formulating tasks for external contractors and coordinating with them to evaluating marketing proposals, checking team expertise, and controlling execution quality.

- **Focus:** Marketing proposals, contractor evaluation, quality control
- **Process:** Weekly calls, quorum-based assessment (3+ members), 2-week review deadlines
- **Contact:** [@vkatsman](https://telegram.me/vkatsman) for observers

---

### [Gonka Restitution Committee](./gonka%20restitution%20committee/README.md)

Handles compensation for losses caused by protocol bugs and unpredictable behavior. Pre-filters protocol-related claims and groups incidents for general votes.

- **Founded by:** [@votkon](https://telegram.me/votkon)
- **Language:** English (inclusive)
- **Join:** Open to technically knowledgeable participants

## How to Participate

1. **Vote on proposals** — Review committee assessments and cast your vote
2. **Join a committee** — Reach out to committee leads (most accept observers)
3. **Start a discussion** — Share ideas in the relevant discussion category
4. **Submit a proposal** — Use committee feedback to strengthen your proposal before on-chain voting
5. **Report issues** — Help improve the protocol by reporting bugs or suggesting features
