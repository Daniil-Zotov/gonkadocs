## 📝 Описание

**QUN × Gonka — exploring collaboration on accessible distributed compute**

Hello Gonka community!

I'm developing QUN, an ecosystem of open creative and collaborative tools. One of our main priorities is building a distributed compute network that makes useful computation more accessible.

We are exploring browser-based compute participation, verifiable task execution, safe workload scheduling, and a Telegram Mini App where users can voluntarily contribute resources and track confirmed work.

We understand that Gonka's Host infrastructure has different hardware and reliability requirements, and we don't want to confuse browser compute with production-grade GPU inference.

We'd like to learn from the Gonka ecosystem and explore practical ways to collaborate:

- understand the current Host and developer participation paths;
- study verification, workload accounting, and incentive mechanisms;
- identify suitable open-source tools or integration opportunities;
- contribute to testing, documentation, or ecosystem tooling where we can provide measurable value.

Our current goal is to build a small, transparent, verifiable prototype first and share real results rather than make unsupported claims.

Would the community be open to a technical discussion about possible integration or collaboration?

Thank you!
