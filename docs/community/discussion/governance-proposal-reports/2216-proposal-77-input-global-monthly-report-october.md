Hi Team, happy to share our October 2026 Monthly Report for Gonka! You can find a detailed breakdown of our results in the report here: https://www.figma.com/deck/U9MT0A9Y22SaeBAaISTvM8

This month we delivered **5 unique pieces** across MENA business, global finance and US tech media, reaching a **combined UVM of 1.4M**. We also converted TOKEN2049 into four separate assets, and have added Share of Voice tracking against the competitor set from this month.

_Secured this month:_
• **Entrepreneur Middle East**, a top tier regional business title, published David's op-ed "Sovereign AI Will Run Faster on Shared Compute". He argued that the Gulf's long-term advantage will come from how efficiently its compute is used rather than how much of it is built — reducing idle capacity, sharing infrastructure across borders, and keeping sensitive workloads on national systems while less-sensitive tasks run on shared networks. 
• **The World Financial Review** published Anastasia's op-ed "You Can Adopt AI Everywhere and Still Not Control Any of It", arguing that owning GPUs does not equal controlling compute, and that the answer is diversified access — domestic infrastructure, shared regional resources and commercial cloud, with privacy-preserving technology protecting sensitive models and data.
• **Grafa**, the Australian finance media-tech platform, recorded an interview with David and Daniil during TOKEN2049 in Singapore on how existing hardware worldwide can be pooled into a decentralised network. It went live on YouTube, X and the Grafa site, and the outlet will reference it as a recap in its TOKEN broadcasts next year.
• Anastasia appeared on **A Global Tech Podcast** during Tech Week in San Francisco, explaining that new GPUs are hard to acquire, run and connect while a great deal of compute worldwide sits unused or underused. The full episode is on YouTube with clips across the show's social channels.
• **The European Business Review** featured Anastasia's expert comment in "What Leaders Should Never Delegate to AI", where she made the case for keeping quality assessment in human hands — technically competent output can still lack taste, craftsmanship and originality.
