## **Why All-In Summit Matters for Gonka**

All-In is one of the few major U.S. technology platforms where decentralized AI has already received significant attention.

### **Bittensor Precedent**

The podcast has covered Bittensor, one of the best-known projects in the decentralized AI category and a direct reference point for assessing the importance of access to the All-In audience.

In October 2025, All-In host **Jason Calacanis** discussed Bittensor and TAO on the podcast and publicly disclosed that he is a partner at Stillcore Capital, a fund that purchases TAO and invests in the Bittensor ecosystem. In March 2026, **Chamath Palihapitiya** brought up Bittensor again, this time directly in a conversation with **Jensen Huang**. He described a decentralized training run on Bittensor Subnet 3 and called it a significant technical achievement. Huang responded positively to the broader distributed AI approach and compared it to a modern version of Folding@home.

Following the release of that episode, TAO showed a sharp positive price movement. Over the following day, various market sources reported gains of approximately **15–20%**, and by the end of March TAO had risen from roughly **$180 to more than $330**, or approximately **90% over the course of the month**. CoinDesk cited the public discussion of Bittensor by Jensen Huang and Chamath Palihapitiya on All-In as one of the factors behind the March rally, alongside Bittensor’s own technological progress and other market catalysts.

This example demonstrates that **All-In can bring decentralized AI out of the narrow crypto segment and directly into conversations with some of the most influential figures in the global AI industry,** generating significant attention around a project.

For Gonka, this is particularly important right now. Bittensor is already present in the All-In information space, while Gonka currently has significantly less exposure there. The physical presence of Gonka’s co-creators at the summit therefore represents an opportunity to begin closing this gap.

## **Goals for David and Daniil**

The primary objective of Daniil and David’s participation is to use the summit as a platform for direct business development and relationship building.

Priorities:

1. Make maximum use of the presence of AI and infrastructure industry leaders to establish personal connections and discuss Gonka.  
2. Present Gonka as an alternative approach to decentralized AI infrastructure, based on compute-weighted governance and productive AI compute.  
3. Establish or strengthen relationships with NVIDIA, Microsoft, and other companies connected to AI infrastructure, hardware, and compute.  
4. Establish direct contact with the All-In team and hosts, creating opportunities for future discussion of Gonka on the podcast or through other All-In formats.  
5. Meet with investors, founders, and infrastructure players who could potentially become network participants, partners, or users.

This is not a sponsorship package and does not involve purchasing guaranteed speaking slots or media coverage. The value of participation lies specifically in giving two Gonka co-creators access to All-In’s carefully selected in-person community and the opportunity to conduct direct discussions.
