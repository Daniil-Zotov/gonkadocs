# Proposal: Gonka at HSC Asset Management Singapore, 8 October 2026

HSC Asset Management proposes that the Gonka community allocate **15,000 USDT** from the Community Pool for a fireside chat on AI for Gonka's founders at HSC Asset Management Singapore, a one-day institutional conference on 8 October 2026 at Fairmont Singapore.

The package is one event, paid once, with no retainer and no renewal. Every deliverable is checkable after the event, and we propose that the Gonka Marketing Committee verifies them.

## Why this event, for Gonka

**The room.** More than 2,000 people are registered and 69 speakers are published across two stages and 24 sessions. The speaker list includes HSBC, UBS, Fidelity International, Invesco, M&G Investments, Pantera Capital, Binance, Tether, Galaxy Digital, Chainlink, ZKsync, Offchain Labs and Animoca Brands. That is allocators and infrastructure operators in the same building on the same day.

**The slot.** Gonka's founders get a 20-minute fireside chat of their own, on AI, on the Global Stage from 16:05 to 16:25. The slot is open on the published agenda today. The founders hold the stage for the full 20 minutes, with an HSC moderator guiding the conversation.

**The timing.** HSC Asset Management Singapore runs in TOKEN2049 week. Under #99 the community already funded Gonka's presence at TOKEN2049 on 7 and 8 October, so Gonka is funded to be in Singapore that week. This proposal adds a stage and a set of meetings to a trip already paid for, and requests no travel or accommodation.

## What is included

**On stage**

- A 20-minute fireside chat on AI for Gonka's founders, a slot of Gonka's own on the published agenda. Gonka names the speakers and the angle, HSC provides the moderator and agrees the questions in advance
- Full recording and photographs, handed over as files Gonka owns and can publish

**In the room**

- Five or more pre-arranged B2B meetings with family offices, asset managers and institutions, scheduled before the event. Gonka supplies the target profile
- A private dinner seat, closed room
- Placement on the A-list of speakers and VIP guests

**On the brand**

- Logo on the digital intermission slides between sessions
- Brand presence across conference banners at the venue
- Premium placement on the event website and registration page, from confirmation to event day

**After**

- Post-event BD support on the contacts not reached on the day
- Access to the event's contact list on an opt-in basis, and introductions from HSC's partner network

## How the price compares

The community has already priced single-event presence twice this quarter.

| Proposal | What it bought | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| #103, passed | Reimbursement of two attendee passes to All-In Summit, 13 to 15 September | 15,500 USDT |
| #99, passed | TOKEN2049 Singapore Gold Sponsorship plus travel, accommodation and operations | 100,000 USDT |


## Who is proposing

HSC Asset Management runs the event itself. The stage, the agenda, the private dinner and the network are its own, so this is not resold access. The event page lists 20 previous editions, from 2024 to September 2026.

## Payment and administration

- Funds move only if the on-chain vote passes, directly from the Community Pool to the recipient address above
- The amount is fixed at 15,000 USDT. There is no conversion, no GNK leg and no exchange-rate exposure for the community
- HSC confirms the slot and puts the brand assets live within two days of the vote passing

## Use of funds and limits

- The 15,000 USDT pays for the package listed under "What is included" and nothing else
- This proposal covers one event, HSC Asset Management Singapore on 8 October 2026
- It does not approve any future HSC event, renewal or additional payment. Any further engagement needs its own proposal

## KPIs and reporting

Set before the event. None of these are outcomes HSC controls, so they measure delivery rather than results.

| KPI | How it is checked |
|---|---|
| The fireside happened, in the slot agreed | The published agenda and the recording |
| Gonka's own speakers and topic | The recording |
| Five or more meetings | Written report naming each counterparty, booked and held counted separately |
| Brand live for the full window | The event site and registration page |
| Recording and photographs delivered | Files handed over within 10 working days |
| Contact report delivered | Names every contact made and what each asked for, within 10 working days |


## Requested vote

- **Yes:** allocate 15,000 USDT from the Community Pool to HSC Asset Management for the package above
- **No:** do not allocate

Deposit: 500 GNK, the current minimum. Voting period: 48 hours.
