<div class="issues-content" markdown="1">
On mainnet, the StoreCommit a host sends in the block before the exchange deadline often doesn't make it into the deadline block. It expires (`timeout_height` is the deadline), and the stage keeps the count committed a block earlier, about 3% below what the host had generated by then. In regular PoC that count becomes epoch weight. In confirmation PoC it is a reading, and `ConfirmationWeight` keeps the lowest reading of the epoch.

Measured on every successful `MsgPoCV2StoreCommit` of epochs 464–467 (4 regular PoC stages, 7 confirmation PoC events):

| | readings (host × model) | last commit landed before the deadline block | count short by (mean) |
|---|---|---|---|
| regular PoC | 139 | 15 (11%) | 3.0% |
| confirmation PoC | 237 | 91 (38%) | 3.3% |
| of which epoch 466 events 1–3 | 105 | 84 (80%) | 3.3% |

No commit landed after a deadline block, and none failed in DeliverTx, so these are commits that never got in. The deadline blocks of epoch 466 events 1–3 all came from one proposer (`DC5C5FF1D8…`) and carried 12, 4 and 6 transactions, against 38–39 in the block before.

Mid-stage the same delay costs nothing: in the two stages I checked, 8% and 11% of commits landed two blocks after the height they were sent at, which is the "block without a commit, then a block with two" in #2201. At the deadline there is no next block.

**Cause.** `CommitWorker.run` acts only on its interval ticker (`poc_commit_interval_seconds`, 5 s by default), and mainnet blocks take ~5.7 s. The tick that first sees block D−1 can fire at any point in that block. When it fires late, the transaction reaches the proposer of D after the proposal is built. Slow proposers shorten that window: of the commits aimed at a block `DC5C…` proposed, 38% came a block late, against 12% or less for most proposers.

A second, smaller gap: the commit reads `GetFlushedRoot()`, the root of the last periodic flush. That flush runs on its own 5 s ticker (`StartPeriodicFlush`), so the final commit can leave out up to one interval of artifacts the ML nodes already delivered.

**Proposed fix** (dapi only, I have a PR ready):
- `run()` also polls the phase tracker every 250 ms and ticks as soon as a new block height appears. The interval ticker stays for retries. `tick` still sends at most one StoreCommit per observed height (one per model in the final window), so the number of transactions doesn't change.
- `maybeSubmitCommit` flushes each stage store before reading its flushed root, as `submitWeightDistribution` already does.

The commit worker code involved is the same in `release/v0.2.16-post3`, `upgrade-v0.2.17` and `upgrade-v0.2.18`.

Related: #2201 (two commits in one block in the final window), #2186 (noise in confirmation readings). Epoch 466 ended with 3.2% of its weight trimmed from `confirmation_weight` (18 of 23 hosts), epoch 465 with 1.5%. I haven't reconstructed per-event readings, so I don't claim how much of that the misses caused.
